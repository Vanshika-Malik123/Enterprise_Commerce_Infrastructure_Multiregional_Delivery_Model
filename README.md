# Enterprise_Commerce_Infrastructure_Multiregional_Delivery_Model
Interactive Power BI analytics solution for evaluating enterprise commerce performance across multiple regions, products, customers, profitability, and shipping. Transforms transactional data into actionable business insights through KPI-driven dashboards, data modeling, and interactive visualizations.


## 📊 Project Overview

The Enterprise_Commerce_Infrastructure_Multiregional_Delivery_Model is an interactive Power BI business intelligence project designed to analyze sales performance, profitability, customer behavior, product performance, and regional trends.

The project transforms transactional sales data into meaningful business insights through interactive dashboards, KPIs, filters, and visual analytics.

The objective is to provide a clear view of business performance and support **data-driven decision-making** across different regions, product categories, customer segments, and sales channels.

---

## 🎯 Business Problem

Organizations generate large volumes of transactional data, but raw sales data alone does not provide an efficient way to understand business performance.

This project addresses the need for a centralized analytical dashboard that can help answer questions such as:

* How are sales and profitability performing?
* Which regions contribute most to overall performance?
* Which product categories and sub-categories perform best?
* Which customer segments generate the most business value?
* What are the major sales and profitability trends?
* How do different shipping and payment methods contribute to transactions?
* Which areas may require further business attention?
